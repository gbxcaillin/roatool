ROA Generator

Rebalance Records of Advice for BT Panorama model clients. Everything runs in your browser; no client data is uploaded anywhere.

1. Load your files

Drop in the ROA workbook (W23_ROA_Client_Details_Rebalance_SOA.xlsx). It holds the client details, recommended models and current holdings, so that is all you need. A Panorama holdings CSV is optional and overrides the holdings in the workbook.

2. Settings (defaults are set from the PDS and your instructions)

3. Review and choose accounts

Each client is moved to the recommended model on the Rebalance list tab (any adviser override applies). Their platform portfolio is their risk profile. Check the flags, change a model if needed, untick anyone you want to leave out.

4. Create the ROAs

Creates one Word file per client, named “First Last ROA”, plus a batch summary spreadsheet, in one zip saved to your Downloads folder. Upload the files to Google Drive and open them with Google Docs.
